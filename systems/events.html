Module

Events.

Oneway Events lets you post events, see who's going in the Oneway App, and check guests in at the door.

Part of Business OS, by contact, in the Closed Selective Server Beta.

Talk to us

Putting it on

Create and run your events.

  • Drafts and publishing

    Save a draft, then publish, start, end or cancel it.

  • Easy edits

    Change the title, times, location or description anytime.

  • Recurring events

    Repeat an event every week, every two weeks or every month. Each date keeps the same local time and its own guest list, and you can change or cancel every upcoming date at once.

  • Capacity limits

    Cap attendance at what the venue holds.

  • Paid tickets

    Sell ticket types with their own price and quantity in the App. Each seat gets its own code.

The people

Know who's coming.

  • RSVPs

    People tap Going or Maybe in the Oneway App and see how many are going.

  • Capacity and waitlist

    Seats include the guests people bring, and the limit is never exceeded. When a spot opens, the next person on the waitlist gets it.

  • Sign-up forms

    Ask your own questions, let people add guests, and approve each sign-up. Everyone who signs up is added to your customers.

  • Guest list

    See everyone who RSVP'd.

  • Check-in

    Check in each guest with one tap, or undo it.

  • Check-in by scan

    Every ticket and guest pass has a QR code. Scan it or type it, and each one checks in once.

  • Live attendance

    How many people are here compared with how many said they'd come.

Reach

Reach more people.

  • Promotion by other businesses

    Let other businesses on Oneway promote or list your event, or open it to everyone.

  • Where it was shared

    Every place your event was shared, and the result from each.

The difference

Events people actually see.

Most event tools give you a ticket page. Oneway puts your event in the App people already use.

  • In the App

    Your event shows on your Center's page, on the map, and in your followers' feeds.

  • Shared by other Centers

    Other businesses can promote your event, with your OK.

  • Real attendance

    See who actually came, next to who said they'd go.

How it works

From a draft to the door.

  1. Create it

    Draft it, then publish.

  2. People RSVP

    Going or Maybe, in the App.

  3. It gets shared

    Other businesses promote it.

  4. Check-in at the door

    Guests checked in as they arrive.

Who it is for

For anyone who hosts events.

  • Venues and clubs

    Every night of the week.

  • Hotels and restaurants

    Dinners, music and holidays.

  • Communities

    Meetings, classes and festivals.

  • Organizations

    Events for members.
