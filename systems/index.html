Built today

Every System, and what it does.

  • Customer RelationshipsCustomer profiles with their full history, deals and pipelines, follow-ups and reminders, companies, saved lists, contact preferences, import and export.
  • InventoryStock by location and shelf, counting by scanning, lots and expiration dates, purchase orders and deliveries, recipes and food cost, and sales that update stock.
  • Asset ManagementA list of everything you own, check-out and return, warranties, condition and downtime, audits, custom groups and cost of ownership.
  • MaintenanceWork orders by priority, requests from the App, preventive maintenance by date and usage, inspections, vendors, reserved parts and labor hours.
  • SchedulingWeekly schedules, open shifts filled automatically, swaps and time off, a time clock, payroll export and labor cost vs. sales.
  • ReservationsRooms, tables, courts and spaces booked from the App, with booking hours, who can book, a bookings list for staff and empty time to fill.
  • Rewards & MembershipTiers and perks, credits earned and spent, member cards in the App, and cards from other programs.
  • Community ManagementMembers, join requests you can approve or decline, invitations, and who joined this week. Free on Oneway OS.
  • Hotel OperationsRooms, rates and group blocks, the front desk and folios, house accounts, housekeeping, guest notes and the night audit.
  • Restaurant OperationsTables and the floor plan, the waitlist, seating and table status, and the 86 list.
  • Property ManagementUnits and leases, rent charged and paid, late fees, renewals, notices and move-outs.
  • Casino OperationsSlot machines and tables on the floor, hand pays, and each guest's limits and exclusions.
  • HVAC Service OperationsCalls and dispatch, your techs' day on their phones, equipment history, service agreements, a price book and good, better, best quotes.
  • EventsCreate and edit events, RSVPs in the App, tickets, capacity and waitlists, check-in at the door, and promotion by other businesses.
  • CommunicationsOne shared inbox, saved and automatic replies, and notes to your team.
  • MarketingOffers, one post to every platform with photos sized for each, campaigns with a calendar, and a check of where your business info is out of date.
  • PeopleRoles that control what each person can do, and permit reminders before they expire.
