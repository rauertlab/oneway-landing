Hotel Operations.

Hotel Operations runs a hotel's rooms, rates and guests from one place, from the booking and the front desk to the night audit and housekeeping.

Part of Business OS, by contact, in the Closed Selective Server Beta. What is still being built is marked Planned.

Talk to us

The book

From the rate to the room.

  • Stays

    Booked by room and dates; what is left to sell, by type and night.

  • Rates

    A rate ladder per room type; a type closed to sale on chosen dates.

  • Group blocks

    Rooms held to a cut-off, released after, tied to the group's event.

  • Taxes and terms

    The room taxes a stay is charged, and the registration card's words.

  • The book you have today

    An export from your current system, brought in as stays.

  • Online travel agencies

    Rooms and rates sent to booking sites, bookings back.

    Planned

Front desk

The desk's whole day.

  • Arrivals, in house, due out

    Every room's state beside the guests.

  • Check in, check out, move

    Deposits recorded, rooms moved before or during the stay.

  • Charges

    Posted by the desk, or waiting from the point of sale for one press.

  • The night audit

    Each night's room charges and taxes, posted by your rules.

  • The numbers

    Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR beside the same night last year.

Folios and accounts

Every charge, and who pays it.

Oneway records money and never moves it.

  • Routing

    A stay's charges sent to a house account or another folio.

  • House accounts

    Billed as numbered invoices, paid oldest first, closed.

  • Disputes and write-offs

    Each with who and why.

  • Split a bill by payer

    Separate windows on one folio.

    Planned

  • Recurring and batch charges

    A charge that repeats, or one charge to many accounts.

    Planned

Housekeeping

Clean, inspected, and known.

  • Room status from a phone

    Dirty, cleaned, inspected, ready; a room sells only once inspected.

  • Rooms to clean

    Each assigned to a person, on their phone.

  • Out of order

    Taken out of sale with its reason, and returned to service.

  • Traces

    A note about a stay for a department on a day, marked done.

  • A guest reported

    What happened, to whom and where, handed to the department that answers it.

Runs on Asset Management

Every room is an asset.

Hotel Operations is its own System, with its own install, screens and acts. Its rooms are assets, so condition, history and upkeep are the same records engineering works on.

  • Out of order is not out of service

    Out of order leaves what can be sold; out of service stays in the count.

  • Brings what a hotel needs

    Reservations, Maintenance and Oneway CRM come with it.

How it works

A stay, from booking to the audit.

  1. Booked

    A room, dates and a rate.

  2. Checked in

    The deposit on the folio.

  3. In the house

    Charges posted, traces worked.

  4. Audited

    The night posted, the numbers read.

Who it is for

Hotels run from one place.

  • Independent hotels

    The book, the desk and the accounts in one System.

  • Resorts

    Hotel, restaurants and casino on one Center.

  • Groups of hotels

  • Inns and boutique hotels
