Module

Scheduling.

Oneway Scheduling builds your weekly schedule, lets staff swap shifts and request time off from their phones, and tracks hours and labor cost.

Replaces 7shifts or Homebase.

Plus what they can't do, because it's connected to the rest of your business.

  • Labor cost vs. sales

    See labor as a percent of sales, using the sales your POS already sends to Oneway.

  • Staffing based on bookings

    Schedule the right number of people each day, based on your reservations and events.

  • One staff list

    Your staff and their roles live in one place, with no separate employee list to keep up.

Part of Business OS, by contact, in the Closed Selective Server Beta.

Talk to us

The week

Build and publish the week.

  • Shifts by role

    Set each shift's hours and role, and edit freely until you publish.

  • Open shifts

    Post a shift with nobody on it and let qualified staff claim it.

  • Publishing

    Once published, each person sees their own schedule.

  • Availability

    Staff set when they can work.

  • Copy last week

    Copy a week's shifts into next week as drafts.

  • Shift reminders

    Staff get a reminder within two hours before their shift.

  • Auto-fill open shifts

    Open shifts go to the first person who's free, rested, available and under overtime.

The team

Swaps and time off, approved by a manager.

  • Shift swaps

    Staff can give away or swap shifts, and a manager approves the change.

  • Cancel a shift

    Cancel a published shift and the person is notified.

  • Time off requests

    Staff request time off, a manager decides, and approved time off is blocked from the schedule.

  • Team notes

    Post a note to the team and see who's read it.

  • Who's working

    See everyone on shift right now.

Time and cost

Hours and labor cost.

  • Time clock

    Staff clock in and out. Managers can fix or void a punch, and it's logged.

  • Timesheet approval

    Managers approve each week's hours.

  • Pay rates

    Set each person's hourly rate and when it starts. Only owners can change it.

  • Labor cost vs. sales

    Daily and weekly labor cost, and labor as a percent of sales.

  • Staffing to demand

    Compare how many people you've scheduled with how many your bookings, events and forecast sales call for.

  • Payroll export

    Export hours, overtime, rates and cost per person as a spreadsheet for your payroll provider.

  • Location check at clock-in

    Optionally require staff to be on site to clock in. Their location is never stored.

How it works

How a week works.

  1. Build the week

    Add shifts by role and leave some open.

  2. Publish it

    Everyone sees their own schedule.

  3. The team adjusts

    Claims, swaps and time off, approved by a manager.

  4. Track hours and cost

    Hours clocked, approved and compared to sales.

Who it is for

For any team that works shifts.

  • Hotels

    Front desk, housekeeping and maintenance, around the clock.

  • Restaurants and bars

    Front and back of house, scheduled against sales.

  • Venues

    The crew each event needs.

  • Shops and services

    Every opening hour covered.
