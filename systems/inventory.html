Inventory Management.

What you hold, where it is, what it cost and what is running out, in one history every department writes to.

Stock

Every item, counted the same way.

  • Items, packs and variants

    A unit, pack sizes, a SKU and a barcode; sizes and colours each counted on their own.

  • By place and by shelf

    Stock where it actually sits, moved between places in one step.

  • Counts

    A whole place or item by item; the difference shown at once, booked by an owner.

  • Waste with a reason

    Spoiled, damaged, expired, lost or used in house, each in dollars.

  • One history

    Every change kept for good; on hand is never typed over.

  • Lots and expiry

    Each delivery's lot and the day it expires.

    Planned

Ordering

Running low, before it runs out.

  • Par and reorder points

    Per place or for the whole business; owners told once when it runs low.

  • Orders drafted for you

    What is low, onto its supplier's order, filled to par and rounded to the pack.

  • Approved, received, closed

    An owner approves; it arrives in part or in full; what never came is said.

  • Suppliers measured

    Their stated lead time beside the real one, fill rate and on-time count.

  • Sending orders to suppliers

    A purchase order by email or PDF.

    Planned

  • A demand forecast

    When each item will run out at today's pace, and when to order.

Where it goes

Used by the work that used it.

  • Parts on a work order

    Their cost lands on the repair, and on the thing it fixed.

  • Parts installed in things

    A part's one life: in stock, installed, removed, repaired or disposed of.

  • Sold out, everywhere

    A product at zero shows Sold out in the Shop and the App, and returns with new stock.

  • Sales from your point of sale

    Square, Shopify, Clover, Toast and Lightspeed sales take stock by SKU.

    Planned

  • Every property at once

    On hand, low items and orders across properties, and stock sent between them.

  • Recipes and plate cost

    What a dish uses, and what it costs to make.

    Planned

The difference

One stock for the whole business.

Kitchen tools count the kitchen; maintenance tools count parts. Here every department writes to one history.

  • Tied to the work

    A part used on a repair is on that repair, and on the thing it fixed.

  • Tied to the customer

    What is out reaches the Shop and the App, not only the storeroom.

  • Every item is a thing you own

    Stock lives in Asset Management's register, on the same map.

How it works

A movement, every time.

  1. It arrives

    Received against its order, at the invoice's cost.

  2. It is kept

    On a shelf in a place, counted when you count.

  3. It is used

    By a sale, a department or a repair.

  4. It is replaced

    Below par, an order is drafted for you to approve.

Who it is for

Anyone who keeps things on a shelf.

  • Hotels

    Linen, amenities and engineering's parts.

  • Restaurants and bars

    Food and drink, counted and costed.

  • Shops

    What is for sale, and what is left.

  • Service companies

    The parts every truck carries.

Know what you have before you run out.
