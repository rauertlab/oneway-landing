Module

Maintenance.

Oneway Maintenance turns every problem anyone reports into a work order, keeps preventive upkeep on schedule, and shows what each repair cost.

Part of Business OS, by contact, in the Closed Selective Server Beta.

Talk to us

Work orders

Every problem, one work order.

  • Open, hand on, close

    Opened, assigned and told, closed with what fixed it, its cost, parts, hours and pictures.

  • How urgent

    Low, medium or high, ranked in the queue.

  • On hold

    Waiting on a part or a vendor, said, never lost as in progress.

  • The board

    To do, started and on hold, moved by hand.

  • Requests

    Reported from the Oneway App by guests, members and staff, with a picture, each answer told.

  • Steps on the job

    A checklist's steps on the work order, ticked, and Done refused while one is left.

Upkeep

Before it breaks.

  • Preventive by time

    Every so many days, with a lead, never piling up; on-time compliance said.

  • Preventive by meter

    Every so many hours or uses, read by a person or by the equipment's sensor.

  • Inspections

    A checklist walked and finished, lease inspections included.

  • Incidents

    One thing that keeps failing, read as one incident with what fixed it last time.

Who does it

The right hands, at the right cost.

  • The technician's phone

    Their work, Start and Done, what fixed it, hours and pictures, offline steps kept.

  • Vendors

    Sent with a not-to-exceed, accepted or declined, done with its cost.

  • Parts from Inventory

    Parts used come off the shelf, their cost on the repair.

  • Escalation

    Work nobody takes is raised, by rules the Center sets.

  • Hours from the clock

    Done fills the hours from its Start, to the quarter hour, unless hours are said.

The difference

Maintenance on the same map as everything.

A maintenance app keeps its own list of equipment. Here work lands on the Center's own things.

  • Every thing is a thing you own

    Work lands on Asset Management's register: downtime and cost of ownership per thing.

  • Requests from the App

    Guests and members report a problem where they already are.

  • Parts from Inventory

    The shelf and the repair are one history.

How it works

From reported to fixed.

  1. Reported

    From the App, the desk or a schedule.

  2. Assigned

    To a person or a vendor, told.

  3. Done

    What fixed it, the parts, the hours and the cost.

  4. Learned

    Downtime and repeat failures, per thing.

Who it is for

Anyone who keeps a building running.

  • Hotels

    Rooms, the boiler, the elevators and the pool.

  • Rental property

    Units and the tenants' requests.

  • Restaurants

    The kitchen's equipment.

  • Venues and clubs

    Everything the night depends on.

Comes with these Systems

  • Oneway Hotel Operations
  • Property Management System
