Industry System

HVAC Service Operations.

Oneway HVAC runs a heating and cooling company's calls, technicians and jobs, from the first call to the finished job, with every unit's history and service agreement.

Replaces ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro.

And it does what they can't on their own, because every System here shares one set of records.

  • Every furnace is an asset

    Each customer's unit, and the shop's own trucks and tools, keep their history, condition and upkeep in one register.

  • Sent by the schedule's rules

    The suggested tech is the first one on shift and free for the window, read from Scheduling.

  • One customer

    A caller is the Center's one customer record, the same one the CRM keeps.

Part of Business OS, by contact, in the Closed Selective Server Beta. What is still being built is marked Planned.

Talk to us

Calls and dispatch

Every call is a job from the first minute.

  • Take a call

    A returning customer or a new one, the service address kept with them.

  • Four kinds of job

    Service call, maintenance visit, install or estimate, in the caller's words.

  • Arrival windows

    One to four hours on your own clock; moved, and the tech moves with it.

  • Send a tech

    Told on their phone at once; their shift and other jobs checked first.

  • The suggested tech

    The first technician free for the window, on shift, sent in one press by the schedule's rules.

  • The dispatch board

    Each tech's shift and jobs in order, and the jobs still waiting.

  • Dispatch on a map

    Drag a job onto a tech, in route order.

In the field

The tech's day, on their phone.

  • My day

    The shift, and each job with its address and window.

  • Start and finish from the phone

    What was done, the parts used and pictures; the office sees it at once.

  • The customer's sign-off

    Their acknowledgment, typed and timed.

  • Estimates and invoice amounts

    Recorded on the job; Oneway never charges a card.

  • On my way

    The tech's step from the phone; the board says On the way, and the customer is told in the App.

  • A price book

    Line items, and good, better and best options chosen on the job, becoming its estimate and invoice lines.

  • Follow-ups

    A day and what for, on the board when it comes due.

Equipment and agreements

Every unit's history, every renewal on time.

  • Units on record

    Make, model, serial, install year, refrigerant, tonnage and warranty.

  • Last time this happened

    A job on a unit arrives knowing everything it has been through.

  • Service agreements

    A plan, its yearly value, its term and visits, and the units it covers.

  • Visits that book themselves

    Due visits on the board, booked as maintenance jobs on the unit.

  • Renewals

    Agreements ending in 60 days listed, renewed from the day the old one ends.

  • Recurring billing

    Agreements billed on their own.

How it works

From the call to the next visit.

  1. A call comes in

    The customer, the address, the problem.

  2. A tech is sent

    In a window that fits their shift.

  3. The job is done

    What fixed it, on the unit's record.

  4. The next visit

    Booked from the agreement, renewed on time.
