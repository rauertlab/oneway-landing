Rewards & Membership.

Oneway Rewards runs your loyalty program: members earn credits for what they do at your place, move up tiers, and redeem what they have earned.

Part of Business OS, by contact, in the Closed Selective Server Beta. What is still being built is marked Planned.

Talk to us

The program

Your program, your rules.

  • Tiers and benefits

    Each tier named, the credits it takes, and what it gives.

  • Earned for activity

    Credits posted for a stay, a meal or a visit.

  • Redeemed

    Credits spent at the place, kept on the card.

  • Lapsing credits

    Credits that expire after the program's months, by a run the owner checks first.

  • Adjusted with a reason

    A card corrected, the reason kept.

Members

Every member, one card.

  • Enrolled anywhere

    From a customer record, a Oneway person or a name, or joined in the App.

  • Their card in the App

    Each member reads their own card, and chooses what they share.

  • Cards from other programs

    A member links the card they already hold; a provider's export confirms it.

  • Recognized elsewhere

    Whose standing this Center honours, and as what.

  • Offers to a tier

    An offer addressed to members of one tier.

    Planned

The difference

A card people already carry.

A loyalty app is one more app to download. Here the card lives in the Oneway App members already use.

  • In the App

    The member's card, beside the place's page.

  • Their choice

    What a member shares with the place, they decide.

  • Other programs welcome

    A card from another program, linked, never copied.

How it works

From a visit to a reward.

  1. Enrol

    At the desk, from the CRM, or in the App.

  2. Earn

    Credits for what members do.

  3. Rise

    Tiers with their benefits.

  4. Redeem

    Credits spent at the place.

Who it is for

Any place people come back to.

  • Hotels and resorts

    Nights, upgrades and the spa.

  • Restaurants and bars

    Meals and the regulars.

  • Casinos

    Play and comps, beside the casino's own program.

  • Clubs

    Members and their standing.
