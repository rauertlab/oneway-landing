The record

One customer, one story.

Bookings, stays, orders, posts and messages land on the same record, so nobody types a history in.

  • A timeline per customer

    Notes, deals and their moves, stays, orders paid, rewards and messages, newest first.

  • Tags, an owner and your own fields

    Words, numbers, dates and lists of your own, filtered and exported.

  • Consent, said once

    Email, texts and calls each agreed or refused. Do-not-contact stops every campaign and audience.

  • Joined on evidence

    Two records become one only on a confirmed address or id, never on a name.

  • Duplicates merged

    Found by email, phone or name and merged by an owner, with every deal carried over.

  • Auto-tags by rule

    Regulars and big spenders tagged from what they did.

Pipeline

From the first message to the signed deal.

Deals at six stages, each with its chance to close and what it is worth.

  • Deals and stages

    Lead, qualified, proposal, negotiation, won and lost, with value and weighted value.

  • Won and lost, with why

    The last 90 days' win rate, the reasons deals were lost, each owner's share.

  • Follow-ups that land

    A due day and hour, handed to a person who is told, on their own Workspace.

  • Leads from your own book

    People who booked with their account and are not on your list, made customers in one press.

  • Your own stages and several pipelines

    Stages named your way, a pipeline per kind of sale.

The difference

A CRM that does not need feeding.

Most CRMs are only as good as what someone types in. Oneway CRM is fed by the business itself.

  • Part of the business

    Reservations, rewards, orders and messages write to the same customer. Nothing to sync.

  • Their own profile, live

    A customer on Oneway keeps their name and face current themselves.

  • Private by default

    Staff see customers only when their role works with them. Strangers see nothing.

How it works

From what happened to who to call.

  1. Something happens

    A booking, a visit, an order or a message.

  2. It finds its customer

    On evidence: the same address or id.

  3. The record moves

    Its story, its deals and its tags.

  4. The team acts

    Follow-ups due land on the right person.
