What a Center is

A Center is whatever your place needs it to be.

A Center can be:

  • an information page for a place, a landmark or what locals know
  • a social profile with posts and followers, the way Instagram works
  • your business's website, on your own domain
  • a community's home, with members, groups, chat and events
  • a storefront that takes orders and bookings
  • the workspace your team runs the business from

Whichever it is, its page is a canvas its team lays out in Oneway OS, Popit by Popit.
