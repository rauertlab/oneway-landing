What it does

Find it, open it, add it.

  • Map and Explore

    One search, two views: the map, or a feed of places, communities, events and people around you.

  • Place cards

    Tap a pin for its card. Open it to go into the place.

  • Add a place

    From either view, or hold a spot on the map to add what is there.

  • Improve this place

    Already on the map? Add a line, a photo or its website instead of a second one.

  • Waypass

    Who you passed and where, between people who both turn it on.

  • Saved

    Save places into lists, private until you share them.

  • Contribute

    What you added and improved, and what is missing near you.

  • Controls on the map

    You, People, Layers, Near me, Waypass and Help, each one tap away.
