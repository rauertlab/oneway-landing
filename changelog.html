Changelog

What's new on Oneway.

  1. October 28, 2026

    v50.1 — Discovery and everyday tools

    A follow-up to v50 focused on finding places, filling in the map, browsing as a guest, and keeping everyday tools together.

    Discovery and places

    • Searchable map places open as Information Centers, including places whose community information has not been filled in yet.
    • Imported places and places added by people use the same Center records. Finding an existing match opens that Center instead of creating another copy.
    • Nearby places needing information appear in Explore and Contribute without relying on a town Center. The list states its area and when it is only a selection.
    • Add an introduction with a short write, review and confirmation flow. Current information is read before editing it.
    • Public information changes reach other signed-in viewers through live updates. Open pages retain their follow and post controls.
    • Map-place links survive a reload. Both the vector map and basic renderer can open indexed places from a map tap.
    • More place categories can appear on the map, with icons appearing at wider zoom levels. Recorded follows and post counts help active places stand out in crowded views, regardless of ownership or Center type.
    • Information Centers use category artwork and an information marker; adding a picture keeps the category marker in the corner.
    • Imported searchable-place coverage currently includes the supported US regions; missing coverage is stated rather than shown as an empty area.

    Location and Waypass

    • Your location marker moves smoothly between fresh device fixes. Moving around the map pauses camera-follow, and recentering resumes it.
    • Stale locations are marked as last known positions. Watching your own location does not turn on public location sharing.
    • Waypass is opt-in for adults and uses the selected device. It collects encounter cards from other people who also enabled it, showing an approximate place and time rather than precise coordinates. On the web it counts while the page is open.

    Guests and profiles

    • Open the App from the website and browse public Home and the map without signing in.
    • Guest likes count toward the real total. Guests can undo their like, while comments, posting, following and negative reactions remain sign-in actions.
    • Eligible visitors can post as themselves at unclaimed Information Centers without becoming members or gaining authority over the place.
    • Simple public profiles put identity, links and posts together without a redundant Profile view. Their first public posts load from the existing profile read.

    Workspace, Opportunities and refinements

    • Workspace brings together the existing Calendar, email tools and connected business workspace.
    • Opportunities is the destination for the Marketplace, jobs and bookable spaces. Tonight near you appears in Discovery.
    • Calendar views, destination links and phone layouts received additional checks across light, dark and simple presentation.
    • Activity colours, server-rendered icons, link insertion and permission checks received targeted fixes. The boost engine remains disabled; this update does not announce active boosting.
  2. October 26, 2026

    v50

    A big update across Oneway: new safety tools, more privacy choices, and new things in the App and for businesses.

    Safety and security

    • Your account is protected at every step: sign-in, two-step verification, passkeys, and the devices you are signed in on, which you can see and sign out of in Settings.
    • Every post, comment, message, bio and listing passes Oneway's safety check before it shows, so threats, hate and harmful content never reach anyone.
    • Community reports are taken seriously: when several trusted people report the same thing, it is hidden until our team decides.
    • Oneway watches for unusual activity around the clock and responds on its own to things like spam floods, password guessing and automated scraping.
    • Our team has a dedicated safety console, and one switch can tighten the whole platform whenever it is needed.
    • Photos and files are checked before they are stored, and a link in a post always shows exactly where it goes.
    • Anyone can ask for an intimate picture shared without consent to be taken down, and every copy of it is removed.
    • Bookings and checkouts are protected from bots and floods, so real people always get through.

    Privacy

    • Your email address is never shown to anyone else, anywhere on Oneway.
    • A private profile stays private everywhere its posts could appear.
    • Choose who sees when you are online: everyone, the people you follow back, or nobody.
    • Choose how comments with strong words appear to you: shown, blurred with a tap to see, or hidden. Keep your own list of words you never want to see.
    • An edited post says Edited, and only you decide whether others can see what you first wrote.
    • Teen accounts get extra care: a teen's location is shared only with friends, and teens are told clearly what a teen account means.
    • You can change your birthday once in Settings, with a clear check before it is saved.

    New in the App

    • Edit opens the page editor right on your profile or your Center's page. Changes stay a draft until you press Publish, and History keeps your past versions.
    • Free placement: every piece stays exactly where you put it, pieces can overlap, and nothing pushes anything else around.
    • The Marketplace: list an item, people nearby find it and message you, and the chat suggests safe public places and times to meet.
    • Every post and card on Home wears its author's own colour, a person's or a Center's.
    • Discovery's map turns with two fingers like Apple Maps, with a compass, and shows the places near you as cards.
    • Home is faster, and Messages, Create and Me come back exactly as you left them.
    • Follower and following counts are live and exact everywhere, and links in posts can be tapped.
    • Twelve month colours in themes, smooth light and dark mode that follows your device, and one icon set with over 1,500 icons.

    For Centers and businesses

    • Book a table or a room right on a Center's own website, with a confirmation email, a calendar file and a link to change or cancel.
    • Tickets show the truth: free tickets say Free, seats are held fairly, and events read the same everywhere.
    • Join a Center's rewards with one press, and the card lands in your wallet.
    • Selling on Oneway costs a simple 1% fee, with a 10-cent minimum.
    • Arrange a Center's pages into categories that open and close, and choose who sees each one.
    • Choose exactly which Facebook Page, Instagram account or Google listing your Center connects.
    • Oneway OS has a new home screen: what is happening now, today's figures and a tile for each System.

    The website

    • The landing's headline line is new, Oneway OS is marked coming soon on the Business OS pages and every System page, and the footer has a Discovery page and this changelog.
  3. October 21, 2026

    Discovery became its own app inside Oneway, with Waypass and Near here. Messages, notifications and team chat were rebuilt, profiles got one clean layout, Lightbulb got a new camera and drafts, and the App and the OS can now be opened and closed from oneway.center itself.

  4. October 20, 2026

    Platform servers have been put live for the first time, and the first App and landing tests began with pleasant results. We have already started work on the next fix to make Oneway even better.
