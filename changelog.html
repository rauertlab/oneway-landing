Changelog

What's new on Oneway.

  1. October 26, 2026

    v50

    We have reinforced Oneway's security and raised our alertness. v50 is also an update to the whole platform: privacy, the App, Centers and businesses, speed and the website. It covers everything since October 21.

    Security

    • Independent security reviews went over follows, follower lists and Centers eleven times, and every gap they found is closed. Follower and following lists never reveal an email address, and someone you blocked cannot find you through a Center's followers.
    • A private profile's posts stay private through every way into Oneway.
    • A Center can be placed inside another Center only when the other Center's managers agree.
    • Someone you blocked, or a stranger to a followers-only post, can no longer watch its comments arrive live.
    • A personal post's details, search results and reply notifications no longer show an internal id that could be traced back to an email address.
    • A script on a page can no longer turn your sign-in into a key that works elsewhere. Adding a passkey, or approving a TV or Watch from Settings, asks for your password first, and confirming a new account checks the password as well as the email.
    • Every photo is checked from its own header before it is stored or opened, and a file that fails the check is never kept.
    • When a post's video is sent to YouTube, it is fetched only through one guarded path and can never reach our private files or internal network.
    • Nobody can take Oneway's role email addresses at oneway.center, such as postmaster, abuse or noreply.
    • Bookings are protected from floods: people who are not signed in are limited per address and per Center, a request that books nothing costs nothing, and a Center is never refused. One moment written two different ways can no longer be booked twice.
    • Public pages are hardened: an unknown event address no longer makes the server search every Center, and a person who leaves the open web leaves every sitemap on the next read.
    • Every new account gets a random internal id that says nothing about its email address. Existing accounts move over in the next steps.
    • Oneway's admin system: our team has the user list, each account's case, a live security log and Shield mode in one place, so we can act on reports and attacks quickly.
    • Flood protection: one very long message from a guest can no longer freeze the server, and live updates and Popit apps have fair limits per person and per address, so nobody can flood the server with connections or data.
    • Threats, slurs, grooming and other harm are refused before they show, in posts, comments, bios, a Center's words, listings and messages, and the message says exactly what to change. Words that could be harm are held so only the author sees them until they are edited or reviewed. Plain cursing still shows, but cursing at someone is refused in public.
    • Reports that act: when several established people on different networks report a post, comment or listing, it is hidden from everyone but its author until a person at Oneway decides. Reports that look coordinated never count.
    • Oneway watches for attacks and acts on them by itself: password guessing, floods of sign-ups and posts, scraping, a stolen session reused elsewhere and storms of errors are each seen, slowed or paused, and recorded for our team.
    • Shield mode: one press tightens the whole platform during an attack, and our team can lift it the same way.
    • Our servers refuse to start in any unsafe setting, the old system behind Oneway is locked out for good, and a visitor's address is read only from our host's own record.
    • No shared key opens anything on the live platform: every admin action belongs to a named person who has passed two-step verification, is recorded first, and asks their password again before anything that cannot be undone.
    • Sign-up no longer leaves a sign-in key where a page's scripts could read it, a passkey sign-in that is never finished leaves nothing behind, and an appeal link works exactly once.
    • Every secret link a business gives a machine (a data feed, a vendor's job link, a report address) now has its own fair limit, counted only once the link is proven.
    • A copyright notice takes a post down only after the person who sent it confirms it from the email we send, or a person at Oneway accepts it.
    • Removing an intimate picture removes the file itself and every copy, even when its poster took it out of the post first, and a post can show only pictures its sender uploaded here.
    • Connecting Google or Facebook again keeps the exact listing or Page you chose, and never moves your Center onto another business's account.

    Privacy

    • You choose who sees when you are online: Everyone, People you follow back (the default) or Nobody.
    • People who join or work at a Center are counted among its followers, but the list names them only if they choose.
    • At sign-up, someone under 18 is told what a teen account limits and how their data is handled, and confirms they are under 18.
    • You can change your birthday in Settings once, after a clear "are you sure". Every birthday is said back in words, with the age it makes, before it applies, and every existing account is asked once at its next sign-in whether its birthday is right.
    • Where state law requires it (Texas, Tennessee, Mississippi and Georgia), a person at Oneway reviews a birthday change. An ID photo sent for that review is kept sealed and used only for the decision.
    • An account whose birthday is under 13 is paused at once, as the law requires, and Google never lists a minor.
    • A teen's location is shared only with friends or with people they choose.
    • Anyone can ask Oneway to remove an intimate picture shared without consent. A person at Oneway reviews every request and removes a qualifying picture, with every identical copy, within 48 hours, as the TAKE IT DOWN Act requires.
    • You choose what you see: comments with words that could upset can be shown, blurred with a tap to see, or hidden, with blur as the default and hidden always for anyone under 18, plus your own list of hidden words.
    • An edited post says Edited. An edit cannot turn a post into something else, its original is shown only by its author's choice, and held or removed words never come back through it.
    • When someone is reported, our safety records keep only their account's internal id, never their email address.

    New in the App

    • Edit opens the page editor right on your profile or your Center's page. Changes stay a draft until you press Publish, History keeps your last 20 versions, and Home keeps its versions too.
    • Free placement: on your profile, a Center's page and Home, every piece stays exactly where you put it, pieces can overlap, and nothing pushes the others around.
    • The editor is smoother: text boxes and shapes turn, sizes survive a save, layering works, and every drag, resize and turn follows your finger without jumping.
    • The Marketplace starts to work: list an item, people nearby find it and message you, and the chat suggests safe public meetup spots, daylight times and tips for both sides.
    • Online dots show wherever a person appears, not only in Messages.
    • Follower and following counts are live and exact on every screen, and an open list updates as people follow and unfollow.
    • Home: posts come first, your name greets you larger and in your own colour, you can pull down to refresh, and pressing Home again takes you to the top and refreshes.
    • Messages, Create and Me come back exactly as you left them when you move between the dock's buttons.
    • Discovery: the map turns with two fingers like Apple Maps, with a compass; Near here shows the places close to you as cards; Explore shows real places nearby that are not on Oneway yet, each with Add it; and Centers without an information Center carry a hazard mark.
    • Waypass counts from the one phone you choose, like StreetPass.
    • The theme menu has the twelve month colours, one for each month, and No hue wears the current month's colour.
    • Light and dark mode follow your device to your own chosen styles, with a smooth crossfade when it switches, and dark mode text is whiter and easier to read.
    • Phosphor is Oneway's one icon set, with a picker of all 1,512 icons for pages.
    • Tapping a notification marks it read, and Mark all read reads them all.
    • Sign-up: tap the circle to pick your picture and crop it in Oneway's own editor, then choose your key settings once.
    • Settings is cleaner, and Connected accounts is one card per platform, where you choose the account you post as and import from and import with one press.
    • Faster: Home is back on screen in 81 ms instead of 314 when you return to it; Discovery, Create and Me start loading the moment you press them; the App's page is half the size; on a first visit the App's background download is 254 KB instead of 614; profiles and Centers open without the page jumping; and long feeds scroll more smoothly on slower phones.
    • Every post and card on Home wears its author's own colour, a person's or a Center's, in Simple and Popit; your colour is on your own posts and profile.

    For Centers and businesses

    • Book a table or a room right on a Center's own website, signed out. Every booking confirms by email with a calendar file and its own link to view, change or cancel it.
    • Tickets tell the truth: a free ticket says Free beside paid ones, a free seat is taken and given back safely, Google reads ticket offers, and an event can set a minimum age, checked when someone books or buys.
    • One event card, made once with the event and shown the same way everywhere.
    • Join a Center's rewards programme from its page in the App with one press, and the card lands in your wallet in Me.
    • Casino offers reach only people 21 and over, everywhere.
    • A Center's website shows what its records say, an edit in Oneway OS shows on the website at the next load, and a Center's website asks our database 4 to 16 times less on each load.
    • Selling: Oneway's fee is 1%, with a 10-cent minimum per order, and sellers pay the card fees. When a card payment is disputed, the order or tickets wait and the seller is told what, how much and by when; a win restores the sale, and a loss voids it and returns Oneway's fee on the amount lost.
    • A Center's pages sit in categories that open and close, like a Discord sidebar. Its owner or an admin can add, rename, drag, choose who sees each page, remove and undo.
    • Anyone who joins or works at a Center follows it, and its follower count is one number everywhere.
    • Edit on a Center's page carries a quick way into Oneway OS for its operators, and staff without the right can no longer rearrange a business's page.
    • Integrations: a Center sees its accounts elsewhere on one page in Oneway OS, picks exactly which Facebook Page, Instagram account, Business Profile listing or YouTube channel it posts as and imports from, and imports with one press. A reconnect keeps the chosen account.
    • Oneway OS has a new home screen (a greeting with the open state, what is happening now, today's figures in one row and a tile per System), and its Everything page shows every place as tiles in their area's colour.

    The website

    • The landing's headline line is new, Oneway OS is marked coming soon on the Business OS pages and every System page, and the footer has a Discovery page and this changelog.
  2. October 21, 2026

    Discovery became its own app inside Oneway, with Waypass and Near here. Messages, notifications and team chat were rebuilt, profiles got one clean layout, Lightbulb got a new camera and drafts, and the App and the OS can now be opened and closed from oneway.center itself.

  3. October 20, 2026

    Platform servers have been put live for the first time, and the first App and landing tests began with pleasant results. We have already started work on the next fix to make Oneway even better.
