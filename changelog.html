What's new on Oneway.

  1. November 15, 2026

    November 15 update — Home, true offers in every post, and screens in zones

    A larger update to Home, settings and Discovery, to how a business's offers stay true across its posts, and to what a paired screen can show.

    Home and the feed

    • Pill mode is back and is the default for anyone who has not chosen, on phones too.
    • Home scrolls smoothly with many widgets, and widgets slide to the side as the window narrows.
    • Home's first screen has a new greeting and post row, with counts beside the row's icons.
    • Posts, TikTok's included, play inside their frame by themselves and open in a black full-screen viewer.
    • A feed video starts silent, with its own sound and pause buttons on the video, and your sound choice is remembered. Space pauses the large viewer on a computer.
    • Add a photo to Home's What's new? box and keep it while you tap or scroll. A photo-only post lands first in your feed.
    • After you post, one press of the profile button shows the new post first, the right post count and "Make your first post" ticked, and Home's widgets are still there after you scroll.
    • Weather and sports widgets can be added to Home, more than one of each.
    • Your story bar shows notes, a profile shows an online dot, and you change your status from your profile.
    • A newsletter letter keeps its own size, and you can post from Home after the box moves.
    • Posts brought in from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube land on your own profile.

    Sign-in

    • Sign-in and sign-up have one way into the App: "Back to the app" when you came from it, "Check out the app" when you did not. After signing in you land on the page you were on.

    Settings and notifications

    • Hidden words hide those words on Home, in posts, profiles, Centers, search, the bell, message previews, stories, the Marketplace and events, not only in comments.
    • A stranger or a guest sees no count where the author hides counts.
    • The bell tells you more, such as when someone likes a post you liked.

    Centers, Discovery and Opportunities

    • A Center's page has the same default layout as a profile: tap the name, bio or picture to edit, and add, name and arrange its own pages.
    • A Center's page, its place on Discovery and its events show one row of what the place has: Directions, Ride there with Uber, Call, Menu, Order or Book.
    • The Discovery map shows places from further out, and a flick on the map glides further.
    • Launches in Opportunities: startups launch on a weekly list with upvotes, each with its own page and a post on its profile. With nothing posted the page says what it is and shows an example card, and you can filter one list by kind while each launch keeps its place in the whole week.

    The website

    • The front page headline reads "Meet Oneway. The social media that connects more." and the mission page carries the mission line.
    • The globe on the front page turns by itself and spins with a finger or the mouse. It is round on Retina screens and stands alone on phones.
    • Real people show on the front page only when enough of them qualify; otherwise it shows examples, never a mix.
    • Terms, Privacy, Community Guidelines and Safety are rewritten for what this update ships.
    • Pages open without ".html" (/login, /terms, /privacy and the rest), and the old addresses still open.

    For businesses: offers that stay true

    • Change an offer, product or event once and it shows on your paired screen and on every post still to go out.
    • Edit or take down an offer and no scheduled post goes out with the old deal. For a post already out, Today shows one question that you answer right there: Take them down or Leave them up.
    • Change an offer that is already posted on another platform and you are asked once, on a card that names the whole change, before anything changes there. The board does not say Published for a copy that still has the old words.
    • Bring a campaign back in step and Undo puts the old posts back at their own times, or says plainly why it cannot. A campaign whose posts were deleted shows amber with Schedule it again.
    • Shape an ad on one device and find it again in the Marketing room from a phone or as a Marketing teammate, and a box change is never lost to an older open sheet.
    • Open What works to see each of your own posts against your usual, with the number of posts it was measured against, and removed posts shown only as "This post was removed".
    • A staff member outside Marketing who begins a draft post is told it is theirs to change or skip and that Marketing posts it, and Marketing sees who began it.

    For businesses: screens

    • Split a paired TV into a main zone, a side and a bottom strip from the screen's row in Marketing, and give each its own posters, video, words or a clock.
    • A TV left running drops an offer once its last day is over, even with the network down, skips a picture that fails, and shows your own card instead of black when nothing is left.

    For businesses: the OS

    • Ask the Brain "any bookings tonight?" in your own words and get them counted and listed. A hotel sees rooms held beside rooms sold. A restaurant server sees only the tables.
    • Tell the Brain to change something in any words, and it says plainly that nothing was changed and where to do it.
    • Today shows the posts out of step with an offer as one flag, and the question about a post that already went out.
    • Runs > Waiting lists every draft that waits for a yes, the same ones and the same number as Approvals and Now.
    • At a new restaurant, Turn on all shows the same count everywhere, and Ready 1 opens straight on the one automation still waiting. Only a manager adds a table.
    • A restaurant's floor plan shows which course each table is on, and a partly seated table by its half fill and dashed edge.
    • A hotel can send one charge code back to the guest's own folio while the stay's other codes keep going to the company, and front-desk staff read the charge and routing codes.
    • Keep a review at the bookstore and approve, decline or answer it in your own words, with the card, Today and the page agreeing. See and stop automations that are still on, and read Posts and Integrations in plain words.
    • Click around the OS, reload it and open places by link for minutes without being shut out. A link to a page that does not exist leaves you where you were with one plain message. If a real limit is reached the screen counts down the seconds and opens again by itself.
    • Press Make my website, undo it all and read whether your website is on the web, not on the web yet or taken off it. After taking a site down, open it straight on Go live and bring it back as a draft.
    • Phone numbers appear spaced the way people write them, such as (609) 555-0174, on the restaurant floor, the Table ready line, Find and Customers, and Call still dials the same number.
    • Scroll back up on the App page, the OS page or inside the OS and read the wordmark and menu on the page's own sky, with no black bar and no words showing through.
    • What your plan does not include shows as a lock with words. Staff on a plan without Business OS get Ask the owner, which opens Team chat.

    Running Oneway (admin)

    • One search on the admin dashboard finds people and Centers together as you type, and you assign from there.
    • The OS console lists reported people, Centers, posts and messages by what they are, with their state, and Act on it.
  2. November 11, 2026

    November update — Profiles, Messages and operating tools

    A consolidated update bringing recent profile, Discovery and operating-tool work together, with everyday fixes and security hardening.

    Emergency browser repair

    • Restored full App styling and navigation after a release-build failure, including Discovery map styles.
    • Fixed the OS menu failing on a fresh Home load.
    • Embedded videos wait for their card layout before loading instead of being permanently replaced by a link. Existing playback choices and provider restrictions still apply.

    Profiles and everyday use

    • Names use the reading font while custom wordmarks retain their own lettering.
    • Profile pages can be added straight from the page bar with an automatic name, then renamed or arranged through the existing editor.
    • Home's Add menu brings the available widgets together in their existing categories.
    • Reading a conversation clears its unread count in the conversation list and Messages navigation, including other open tabs. Messages arriving while the conversation is in the background remain unread.
    • Home feed pages and less-used operating tools load when opened, reducing the initial download.

    Discovery and media

    • Photos keep their correct orientation in JPEG, PNG and WebP originals and generated sizes. Rotated WebP originals are stored upright for browsers that ignore rotation metadata; image preparation stays bounded.
    • Map place markers retain their category colours in both map styles.
    • Supported fonts, maps and display libraries are served through Oneway's own origin. Picture requests are bounded and legal picture holds also cover cached server copies.
    • Feed pictures retain their aspect ratio while loading, and desktop feed media use the shared media controls.

    Operating tools

    • Hotel work includes room-unit and desk refinements, morning exports, safer queued actions, checkout thank-you handling and complimentary-ticket capacity handling.
    • Restaurant tools include recipe-linked stock availability, receiving, pars and host/pre-service refinements.
    • Pickup payments retain the paid-order fence while operating records catch up, preventing a second checkout during that interval.
    • Connected-work records and the marketing board identify where an item was sent, imported, disconnected or closed.
    • Marketing segments respect recorded opt-outs and verified delivery-provider suppression events.
    • Business signup, founding, claim and plan-return flows share the existing account and payment controls.

    Account and security

    • Passkeys require user verification and are bound to the account, origin and sign-in challenge.
    • Personal automatic Centers stay out of public speaking identities while explicit memberships remain available.
    • Session, shared-canvas, import, booking and outbound-request checks are tightened.
    • Exporting the entire customer list requires the existing higher-trust approval.
    • Requests to speak to a human pause an automatic keyword reply.
  3. October 28, 2026

    v50.1 — Discovery and everyday tools

    A follow-up to v50 focused on finding places, filling in the map, browsing as a guest, and keeping everyday tools together.

    Discovery and places

    • Searchable map places open as Information Centers, including places whose community information has not been filled in yet.
    • Imported places and places added by people use the same Center records. Finding an existing match opens that Center instead of creating another copy.
    • Nearby places needing information appear in Explore and Contribute without relying on a town Center. The list states its area and when it is only a selection.
    • Add an introduction with a short write, review and confirmation flow. Current information is read before editing it.
    • Public information changes reach other signed-in viewers through live updates. Open pages retain their follow and post controls.
    • Map-place links survive a reload. Both the vector map and basic renderer can open indexed places from a map tap.
    • More place categories can appear on the map, with icons appearing at wider zoom levels. Recorded follows and post counts help active places stand out in crowded views, regardless of ownership or Center type.
    • Information Centers use category artwork and an information marker; adding a picture keeps the category marker in the corner.
    • Imported searchable-place coverage currently includes the supported US regions; missing coverage is stated rather than shown as an empty area.
    • Map places missing from the separate search inventory resolve against the actual map source, preserving verified names and points instead of rejecting the place.

    Location and Waypass

    • Location sharing has an explicit save action and confirmed status. Starting sharing returns to the map, shows your position and follows fresh fixes; hiding other people keeps your own marker visible.
    • Your location marker moves smoothly between fresh device fixes. Moving around the map pauses camera-follow, and recentering resumes it.
    • Stale locations are marked as last known positions. Watching your own location does not turn on public location sharing.
    • Waypass is opt-in for adults and uses the selected device. Two people who both turned it on swap cards when they are really near each other, about 150 metres, at the same moment, and are both told at once a few minutes later, with a place name and a time rather than coordinates. On the web it counts while the page is open.

    Guests and profiles

    • Open the App from the website and browse public Home and the map without signing in.
    • Guest likes count toward the real total. Guests can undo their like, while comments, posting, following and negative reactions remain sign-in actions.
    • Eligible visitors can post as themselves at unclaimed Information Centers without becoming members or gaining authority over the place.
    • Simple public profiles put identity, links and posts together without a redundant Profile view. Their first public posts load from the existing profile read.

    Workspace, Opportunities and refinements

    • Workspace brings together the existing Calendar, email tools and connected business workspace.
    • Opportunities is the destination for the Marketplace, jobs and bookable spaces. Tonight near you appears in Discovery.
    • Calendar views, destination links and phone layouts received additional checks across light, dark and simple presentation.
    • Activity colours, server-rendered icons, link insertion and permission checks received targeted fixes. The boost engine remains disabled; this update does not announce active boosting.
  4. October 26, 2026

    v50

    A big update across Oneway: new safety tools, more privacy choices, and new things in the App and for businesses.

    Safety and security

    • Your account is protected at every step: sign-in, two-step verification, passkeys, and the devices you are signed in on, which you can see and sign out of in Settings.
    • Every post, comment, message, bio and listing passes Oneway's safety check before it shows, so threats, hate and harmful content never reach anyone.
    • Community reports are taken seriously: when several trusted people report the same thing, it is hidden until our team decides.
    • Oneway watches for unusual activity around the clock and responds on its own to things like spam floods, password guessing and automated scraping.
    • Our team has a dedicated safety console, and one switch can tighten the whole platform whenever it is needed.
    • Photos and files are checked before they are stored, and a link in a post always shows exactly where it goes.
    • Anyone can ask for an intimate picture shared without consent to be taken down, and every copy of it is removed.
    • Bookings and checkouts are protected from bots and floods, so real people always get through.

    Privacy

    • Your email address is never shown to anyone else, anywhere on Oneway.
    • A private profile stays private everywhere its posts could appear.
    • Choose who sees when you are online: everyone, the people you follow back, or nobody.
    • Choose how comments with strong words appear to you: shown, blurred with a tap to see, or hidden. Keep your own list of words you never want to see.
    • An edited post says Edited, and only you decide whether others can see what you first wrote.
    • Teen accounts get extra care: a teen's location is shared only with friends, and teens are told clearly what a teen account means.
    • You can change your birthday once in Settings, with a clear check before it is saved.

    New in the App

    • Edit opens the page editor right on your profile or your Center's page. Changes stay a draft until you press Publish, and History keeps your past versions.
    • Free placement: every piece stays exactly where you put it, pieces can overlap, and nothing pushes anything else around.
    • The Marketplace: list an item, people nearby find it and message you, and the chat suggests safe public places and times to meet.
    • Every post and card on Home wears its author's own colour, a person's or a Center's.
    • Discovery's map turns with two fingers like Apple Maps, with a compass, and shows the places near you as cards.
    • Home is faster, and Messages, Create and Me come back exactly as you left them.
    • Follower and following counts are live and exact everywhere, and links in posts can be tapped.
    • Twelve month colours in themes, smooth light and dark mode that follows your device, and one icon set with over 1,500 icons.

    For Centers and businesses

    • Book a table or a room right on a Center's own website, with a confirmation email, a calendar file and a link to change or cancel.
    • Tickets show the truth: free tickets say Free, seats are held fairly, and events read the same everywhere.
    • Join a Center's rewards with one press, and the card lands in your wallet.
    • Selling on Oneway costs a simple 1% fee, with a 10-cent minimum.
    • Arrange a Center's pages into categories that open and close, and choose who sees each one.
    • Choose exactly which Facebook Page, Instagram account or Google listing your Center connects.
    • Oneway OS has a new home screen: what is happening now, today's figures and a tile for each System.

    The website

    • The landing's headline line is new, Oneway OS is marked coming soon on the Business OS pages and every System page, and the footer has a Discovery page and this changelog.
