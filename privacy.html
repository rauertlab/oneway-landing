How we use it

  • To run Oneway: sign you in, show your profile, deliver posts and messages, make bookings, and send the notifications you asked for.
  • To keep accounts safe: limit sign-in attempts, require two-step sign-in in casino, guest, rewards and credential areas, and keep the security log.
  • To rank Home from who and what you follow, the places you belong to, your reactions, what you have already seen and what you are watching now.
  • To show friends what you share through StreetPass: Centers you both belong to and events you are both going to.
  • To let places recognise you, if you allow it.
  • To moderate. People decide reports. An automated check can move a report up or down the queue but cannot act on it.
  • To write or suggest text and images when you use an AI feature. What you give that feature is sent to an AI provider to produce the answer. Oneway does not train AI models on your posts or messages, and each provider's own terms govern what it does with what it receives.
  • To meet legal duties.

We do not read your messages to rank posts or show ads. Moderators cannot open conversations. No decision about you is made by automated means alone: recognition only suggests and a person at a business confirms, and an automated check on a report can only change its place in the queue.

Recognition by places

A business keeps a guest list: the hotel you stayed at, the restaurant you booked. Recognition lets it see that a record is you.

  • A match is shown only when the record shares something you have proven: the email you sign in with, or a phone number or account you verified. A name alone never makes a match.
  • A suggestion can appear when a record looks like your name, picture, bio and the words in your newest public posts. Posts for followers or friends only, deleted posts and a private profile's posts are never read. The picture check compares photographs. It notices the same photo used twice. It does not analyse your face.
  • Which businesses: only ones you already have a tie to: their staff, their guests from the past year or with a stay coming up, people who posted there, and their followers.
  • A person confirms. Someone at the business must confirm or reject a suggestion. A rejected one is remembered and not offered again.
  • Once linked, the business sees whether you follow it, your role there, your bookings and posts there, and at a casino, that day's cash activity at the cage.
  • We do not guess health, religion, ethnicity, sexuality, politics or finances from what you share.

Businesses and their records

  • A business on Oneway, called a Center, can keep records about its customers: name, email, phone, its own customer number, a rewards number, a picture, notes, and the customer's names on other platforms. It types them in or imports them from its own systems.
  • The business is responsible for those records. To see, correct or delete one, ask the business. We help it answer. You do not need a Oneway account for a business to hold information about you.
  • When a Center is created, Oneway gathers public information about the organization to propose a starting profile. Nothing is published until its operator confirms it. To correct or remove information about your organization, email us.
  • A Center's Brain keeps a record of how people relate to the Center, built from what happened: follows, bookings, attendance, and posts there. It recommends, and a person at the Center decides. We do not infer health, religion, ethnicity, sexuality, politics or finances.
  • A Center may also keep staff records, such as schedules and hours worked.
  • A Center may also have its own website and its own privacy practices. This policy covers Oneway, not a Center's own site.
  • Hotels and casinos may also keep stays, bills and deposits, rewards balances, cage transactions, limits you set on yourself, exclusion lists, and records the law requires.
  • Your rewards standing moves between businesses only if you turn that on. The business receiving it sees your tier and which business it came from, never your balance, member number or play.
  • When you book, message, join or follow a business, it sees that.

Security

  • Passwords are kept as bcrypt hashes and session tokens as SHA-256 hashes. The sign-in cookie cannot be read by scripts on a page and is sent only to our own site.
  • These are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with a separate key for each business and a master key kept outside the database: connected-account access, two-step secrets, and casino ID and taxpayer numbers.
  • Sign-ins and changes to who can get in are written to a log built so that any later change to it can be detected.
  • Other records, such as posts, profiles and bookings, are ordinary database records. We will state the hosting and disk encryption here before the App opens to the public.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your private information, we will tell you and the authorities as New York and other state laws require. Report a security problem to williamrau78@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia, New Jersey and other states, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to opt out of sale, sharing for advertising and profiling, and to appeal if we say no. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising, and you can turn recognition off at any time.

To use a right, email williamrau78@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, and may need to confirm it is you. You may ask someone to act for you; we will ask for proof they can. Using your rights will not change how we treat you. If we refuse, reply with the word Appeal and someone who did not make the first decision will review it and tell you the result in writing within 45 days. For Facebook and Instagram data, see Data Deletion.

Children

Oneway is for people 18 and older, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.
