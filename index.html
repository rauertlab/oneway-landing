Meet Oneway. The social media that connects more.

Oneway is a new social media and web ecosystem that brings people, places, and communities together through customizable Profiles and Centers. Share posts, message friends, explore interactive maps, and build your own corner of the web with widgets and tools that fit naturally.

Be a guest

Your corner of the internet.

Connected wherever you go.

  1. Bring your social networks, websites, stores, and content together in one customizable Profile, arranged exactly how you want it, with its own link and QR code to share anywhere.

  2. Share a moment, reach new people through our For You page, and instantly post across your other social networks.

  3. Go somewhere? Discover its Center as you arrive, receive a greeting right in the app, or passively exchange Profiles with nearby Oneway users through Waypass.

  4. Book directly through a hotel’s website from its Center, stay in the know with event times, business hours and locations, and RSVP.

Make the web more connected with one Profile that brings everything you want to access and share closer together.

Centers

A place for just about anything.

Your favorite restaurant, a neighborhood across the world, or a community built around something you love. Each can have its own Center on Oneway.

Centers bring together posts, photos, information, conversations, and things to do. Explore a place’s history, see what people are sharing, or create a community of your own.

Every Center is customizable. Arrange its layout, add pages and interactive elements, or build out a website with menus, events, products, and booking options. A simple information page can grow into something much larger as people contribute or an organization takes ownership.

Profiles

Your profile. Your place. Built your way.

These profiles and Centers are examples, until the beta’s own arrive.

More than a social media profile, your own place on the web.

Bring your posts, photos, social accounts, websites, and favorite links together in one shareable Profile. Give people an easy way to discover everything you’re part of, whether it’s on Oneway or somewhere else.

Keep it simple as a personal link hub, or make it entirely your own. Create a custom wordmark, arrange galleries, add pages, and personalize your space with colors, stickers, and interactive elements. Your Profile is a canvas you can keep changing as you go.

The Feed

For You, and you get to choose.

Discover videos, photos, stories, and conversations from your favorite creators, friends, and communities, alongside people and places you haven’t found yet.

Your For You feed brings together your interests, the topics you follow, and activity from the wider Oneway community. Explore what’s happening around the world, follow specific keywords or Centers, and create your own feeds around whatever interests you.

Switch between an immersive, full-screen feed and a traditional scrolling layout. Share your own posts, connect supported social accounts, and change what you see as your interests evolve.

Connecting your everyday

From discovering to doing.

Find a restaurant through Google? Visit its website, explore the menu, and book a table through its existing reservation service.

Maybe a friend sends you an event they found online. Explore the venue, see who’s going, join the conversation, and make plans together.

Or discover something through your own feed, whether it’s a local business, a community, or somewhere you’ve always wanted to visit.

Not everything has to start on Oneway. From websites and social media to the places you visit in person, Oneway brings more of those experiences together.

You might visit the same coffee shop, attend the same events, or pass someone regularly without ever meeting.

Waypass helps people who opt in discover others whose paths have crossed through shared places. Explore their Profiles and choose whether to connect, without making your live location public.

Compatibility & Cross Projection

Your world, connected across the web.

Oneway works alongside the accounts, websites, and services you already use. Connect supported platforms, bring your online presence together, and share content across compatible networks.

For businesses, Cross Projection takes that further.

A venue can create an event, upload its artwork, and prepare it for its Center, website, social media accounts, and digital displays. Connected information can be updated, checked for differences, and published to supported destinations.

Someone discovering the event can book through the venue’s existing website or a supported booking service.

Businesses keep their websites, customer accounts, and existing tools while connecting more of the experience.

Privacy, Safety & Security

Your space. Your rules.

Choose what appears on your Profile, manage connected accounts, and control who can interact with you.

Set conversation rules, manage disappearing messages, and decide whether to participate in location-based features like Waypass.

Oneway also provides blocking, reporting, account protection, and permissions that help keep private business operations separate from public activity.
