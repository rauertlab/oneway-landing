Questions

Oneway, in plain words.

What is Oneway?

Oneway is a social network and a business platform in one. Every person has one profile, and every community, place and business has a Center.

What is a Center?

A Center is the home of a place, a community or a business on Oneway. At its simplest it is an information page; it can also be a social profile, a website on its own domain, a storefront and the workspace its team runs it from.

What is Oneway OS?

Oneway OS is where a Center is run: its page and website, posts, events and community, free. Business OS adds bookings, staff and the Systems of its industry.

Who controls my data?

You do. Your profile shows only what you choose, your work for a Center stays apart from your personal activity, and you can delete your data and your account; if you own a Center, you hand it over first.
