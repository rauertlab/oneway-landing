Questions

Common questions.

  • How do I sign in?

    While the Closed Selective Server Beta runs in New York City, Sign in on oneway.center opens the beta page. There a business can ask to start on Oneway OS, and anyone else can ask for an invitation to the App.

    The beta

  • How do I get Business OS?

    Ask for it from the contact page, and we set it up together.

    Business OS

  • I am a developer. How do I start?

    Tell us what you plan to build, from the developer part of the contact page.

    Developer OS

  • How do I delete my data or my account?

    The Data Deletion page walks through each way to remove what is yours, from one post to your whole account, and says what stays.

    Data Deletion

  • What does Oneway do with my information?

    What we collect, why we use it and who can see it are all set out in the Privacy Policy.

    Privacy Policy

  • How do I block or report someone?

    The Safety page shows where Block, Mute and Report are in the App, and what each one does.

    Safety

  • What is allowed on Oneway?

    The Community Guidelines hold the rules, and our Terms are the agreement under them.

    Community GuidelinesTerms of Service

  • Something on Oneway does not work for me.

    The Accessibility page lists what works today, the gaps we know of, and how to tell us about a barrier.

    Accessibility

  • Who makes Oneway?

    Oneway Technologies LLC makes Oneway, and Our Mission says why.

    Our Mission
