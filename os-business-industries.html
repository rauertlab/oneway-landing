Rental property

The rent roll, unit by unit.

Oneway OS, with sample records.

Every unit leased, on notice or vacant, what is owed past its due date, and the leases coming to an end.

  • Units

    Each unit named as the building names it, with its bedrooms and its asking rent.

  • Leases

    The tenant, the rent, the deposit and the term: in force, on notice or ended. Two leases never overlap on one unit.

  • Statements

    Each lease's rent, fees and credits, and the payments recorded against them. Oneway collects no rent.

  • Past due

    What is owed, oldest first, aged 1⁠–⁠30, 31⁠–⁠60, 61⁠–⁠90 and over 90 days.

  • Renewals

    The leases ending within 90 days, each renewed from where it shows.

  • Move-in and move-out

    A checklist walked at each end of a lease. Once both are done, every item sound at move-in that failed at move-out is named, with both notes.

  • The owner's statement

    A month of the property as its owner reads it: for each unit, what was charged and what was collected.
