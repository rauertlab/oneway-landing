Events

Found, answered and counted.

The Oneway App, with a sample Center.

People find the event on the Center's page in the Oneway App, say Going, Maybe or Not going, and see how many are going.

  • A draft until it is out

    A draft is the team's alone until it is published. A cancelled event stays on the page and says so.

  • Spaces to book

    A room, a court or a lane booked from the App. Where the venue confirms each booking, the guest is told it is a request before sending it.

The work

The work behind every night.

Oneway OS, with sample records.

Work orders on the venue's own things, in the order to do them.

  • Requests

    Staff, members and guests with a booking report a problem, and Maintenance takes it on as a work order or declines it, saying why.

  • Inspections

    A checklist the venue writes, run on a stage, a room or the whole place. An item that fails opens its own repair.

  • Preventive work

    Upkeep that recurs opens its own work order before it is due.

  • Vendors

    Work sent to a contractor, and what they said and what it cost when it came back.

  • Shifts

    Each night's crew clocks in from their Workspace, and before payroll a manager fixes or excuses every late start and missed shift.
