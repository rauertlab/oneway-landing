Data Deletion

How to delete what is yours on Oneway, from one post to your whole account: what goes at once, what is kept, for how long, and why. The Privacy Policy is the full account of what we hold.

ContentsA post or a photoA commentA connected accountA copy of your dataYour whole accountWhat is kept, and whyFor Meta, Threads, Instagram and Facebook
On your profile

A post or a photo

  1. Open the post in the Oneway App
  2. Press Delete, then Delete for good?
  3. It leaves your profile, every feed and every Center it was posted to at once, with its photos and videos.

A photo goes with the post it is in. The files themselves are erased when you delete your account.

Under a post

A comment

  1. Open the post and find your comment
  2. Press Delete beside it
  3. It leaves the conversation at once, and the post's count goes down.
Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X and the others

A connected account

  1. In the Oneway App, open Settings, then Connected accounts
  2. Press Disconnect beside the account, then press it again
  3. The access the platform gave Oneway is deleted straight away, and nothing more is sent there. Where connecting it verified that account on your profile, the verified mark goes with it.

An account connected for a Center you run is disconnected the same way, in Oneway OS under Settings, then Connected accounts.

Posts already sent stay on the platform, where you can delete them. Posts you imported from it stay on Oneway as your posts, and you delete each one like any other post. The platform keeps Oneway in its own list of connected apps until you remove it there too.

Before you delete anything

A copy of your data

In the Oneway App, Settings, then What Oneway knows about you, shows what Oneway understands about you. For a full copy, email williamrau78@gmail.com from the address on your account. We answer within 30 days, and may need to confirm it is you.

Everything at once

Your whole account

  1. In the Oneway App, open Settings, then Delete your account
  2. Enter your password, and the code from your authenticator app if two-step is on
  3. Press Delete your account, then Tap again to delete it for good

If you own a Center, hand it over to someone in Oneway OS first; you are told which one. Your own space is not a Center you run, and it goes with your account.

What goes at once: every session, on every device. Your profile, picture and search listings. Your posts from view, and your reactions, reposts and saves. Your follows, blocks and mutes. Your Center memberships and your own space. Sites you signed in to with Oneway. Photos, videos and files you uploaded. What your Brain kept. The accounts you connected on your profile, with the access we held for them. Your linked identities. Your shared location and your Footsteps. What reached your screen, and what the feed learned from it. Your list of bookings. Your notifications.

It cannot be undone, and nobody can sign in to it again. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on the account.

After you delete

What is kept, and why

WhatHow long, and why
Your email address, the day you joined and the day you leftKept, with no end date, so no one else can register the address and inherit your history.
Comments and messages you left in other people's conversationsThey stay, shown as Deleted account with no picture, because they are part of other people's conversations.
A post or a comment you deletedGone from view at once. Its record stays in our database, so a reply or a share that pointed at it can say it was removed.
What a Center keepsIts own records, its bookings, the pictures uploaded for it and the accounts connected for it stay with the Center: they are its records, not your profile's.
The security logKept after you delete your account, because it is the record of sign-ins and changes to who can get in. We have not set an end date. We will set one and publish it in the Privacy Policy before the App opens to the public.
Casino compliance recordsAt least five years, as the law requires. They cannot be deleted on request.
BackupsWe will state the backup period in the Privacy Policy before the App opens to the public.
Legal holdsLonger only when the law, a regulator or a legal claim requires it, and only for as long as it does.

What Oneway receives from Facebook

When you connect a Facebook Page

  • The access Facebook gives Oneway: a key for your Facebook account and one for each Page you chose. They are kept encrypted and are never shown back to anyone.
  • Each Page's name and ID, and, for the Page Oneway posts to, the ID of the Instagram professional account linked to it, if it has one. Instagram comes through the same Facebook sign-in.
  • If you start an import: the Page's posts, with their photos and videos, copied onto Oneway as posts.

Oneway uses it to send the posts you pick to that Page and its Instagram account, and to copy the Page's posts when you start an import. It never receives your Facebook password, and it does not ask Facebook for your friends or your messages. Threads connects through its own sign-in and gives Oneway its own key, with your Threads account's ID and username.

On Facebook

Remove Oneway from your Facebook account

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy, then Settings
  2. Open Apps and websites (facebook.com/settings?tab=applications)
  3. Find Oneway Services and press Remove
  4. Facebook ends the access it gave Oneway: Oneway can no longer post to your Pages or read them.

What Oneway already received stays with Oneway until you delete it, below.

On Oneway

Delete what Oneway received from Facebook

  1. In the Oneway App, open Settings, then Connected accounts. For a Center, open Oneway OS, then Settings, then Connected accounts
  2. Press Disconnect beside Facebook, then press it again. Do the same beside Instagram
  3. Oneway deletes the access it held for them straight away, with each Page's name and ID, and nothing more is sent to them.
  4. Posts you imported from Facebook are Oneway posts: delete each one like any other post.

Deleting your Oneway account deletes the Facebook and Instagram connections on your profile too. Or email williamrau78@gmail.com from the address on your Oneway account with the name of the Facebook Page, and we delete what Oneway received from Facebook for it. We answer within 30 days, and may need to confirm it is you.
