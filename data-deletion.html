Deletion

What we hold when you connect an account, and how to remove it.

What We Hold

When you connect an account

  • The access the platform gave us, kept encrypted and never shown back
  • The account's name and ID, and its picture, shown in Connected accounts
  • For Facebook and Instagram, the Pages you chose and their names
  • If you started an import, the posts we copied onto Oneway. They are your posts.

We do not hold your password for any platform. For Bluesky, we hold an app password you can revoke.

Option 1 — From Oneway

Disconnect an account

  1. Sign in at oneway.center
  2. Go to Settings → Connected accounts
  3. Press Disconnect next to the account
  4. The stored access is deleted straight away, and so is the verified mark it gave your profile.
Option 2 — From the platform

Remove Oneway from your Facebook account

  1. Open Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings
  2. Open Security and Login → Apps and Websites
  3. Find Oneway Services and press Remove
  4. Facebook tells us, and we remove the Facebook and Instagram connection.

Other platforms have the same screen in their own settings.

Disconnecting does not delete your Oneway account, or the posts you made on Oneway. To delete the account, use Settings → Delete your account. The Privacy Policy says what that removes and what stays.
