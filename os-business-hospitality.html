Hotel Operations

The book, from the rate to the room.

Oneway OS, with sample records.

A hotel sells a room type, for a night, at a rate. The book keeps all three, and what is left to sell on every date.

  • Rooms and rates

    Room types, rate plans and what is left to sell, by type and by date.

  • Holds and moves

    A room taken out of sale for dates, with its reason, and a guest moved to another room in the house or before they arrive.

  • Group blocks

    Rooms held for a group until its cut-off date, and the pick-up as its guests book.

  • Deposits and the city ledger

    Deposits recorded as guests put them down, and the house accounts billed instead of the guest. Oneway records money and never moves it.

  • Pace

    The nights ahead on the books, how fast they are coming in, and where last year stood at the same time.

  • Housekeeping and upkeep

    A cleaned room sells only once a supervisor has inspected it. Upkeep follows use too, such as a boiler's run hours or an elevator's trips.

Guest Experience

Every guest, known across the property.

Oneway OS, with sample records.

What each department needs to know about a stay, the guest's own messages, and a program that rewards them for coming back.

  • Traces

    A note about a guest's stay, for a department, on a day. The department sees it in its Workspace that day and marks it done.

  • Rewards

    The hotel's own program: a tier earned within the program year is kept through the next, and the credits a guest spends lapse after months without activity. At a casino resort, the casino's own system keeps the points.

  • One inbox

    Guests' messages arrive in the Center's one inbox.

  • A problem, reported once

    A guest reports a problem in their room from the Oneway App, during the stay or up to 30 days after it. If the room already has work open, the report joins it, and the guest is told when it is fixed.

Front Office

The desk's day, and the numbers.

Oneway OS, with sample records.

Arrivals, the guests in the house and the ones leaving today, the night audit, and the figures every hotelier judges a property by.

  • The board

    Who arrives, who is in the house and who is due out, with each room's state.

  • Check-in and the folio

    The stay begins, the room becomes occupied, and a deposit held meets the folio every charge lands on.

  • The night audit

    Each night's room charges and taxes posted to the folio, by the rules the property names.

  • The numbers

    Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR from what the audit posted, beside the same night last year.

  • Charges to post

    A check charged to a room by a connected point of sale waits for the desk, which posts it in one press. Nothing posts by itself.

  • The book you have today

    A reservations export from the hotel system you use now, or a spreadsheet, brought in as this hotel's stays.

Additional Hospitality Systems

The property's other systems, one intake.

What the hotel system, the points of sale, housekeeping and the valet report becomes one Center's events, one guest's timeline, and work for the person on shift.

  • One system at a time

    Each is connected on its own, in Oneway OS's Integrations, and a system nobody has connected says exactly that.

  • One guest, on evidence

    Two systems' guests are one guest only when the same card, the same address or the system's own guest id arrives from both. A name alone joins nothing.

  • Casino resorts

    Oneway runs beside the casino management system. It reads it, writes nothing to it, and keeps no gaming money.

  • The casino's own day

    The hosts' book of guests and their trips, the floor by area, and the pit's dealer rotation.
