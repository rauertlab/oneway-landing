App

Go anywhere. Connect everywhere.

Follow your friends, discover places you love, and bring your favorite parts of the internet together.

Discovery

Discover people, places, and communities. Your way.

Explore Oneway through an interactive map or a personalized grid of posts. Find local businesses, events, and communities, follow the places you love, and see what is happening around you.

Connect with other Oneway users passing the same local Centers, quietly, with Waypass. You control whether you take part.

Create Info Centers to populate our ecosystem and win monthly rewards and ranking in your area.

Monthly rewards and eligibility rules are planned.

Home

For You, and you get to choose.

Oneway is a customizable social media app for photos, videos, and posts. Explore a ranked For You feed, follow people and places, switch to Following, or create custom feeds that shape what you see.

Import posts from supported connected platforms and arrange Popits on your in-app Home. iOS Home Screen widgets are planned, not available yet.

Messages

Messages, Your Way.

Most messaging apps decide how your conversations work. Oneway lets you and your groups choose the rules.

Choose privacy, membership, posting, and notifications. An admin can manage the chat, or members can vote by majority, two thirds, or unanimous agreement.

Explore messaging controls
  • Keep messages or set them to disappear
  • Turn notices for detected screenshot shortcuts on or off
  • Choose who can add or remove people, post, and change rules
  • Open the group, approve requests, or invite people in
  • Let new members see the chat history or start fresh
  • Show or hide edit history and deleted message markers
  • Turn read receipts, typing, reactions, replies, and forwarding on or off
  • Allow photos and videos or keep the chat text only
  • Set slow mode or leave it off
  • Get every alert, mentions only, or mute the chat
  • Accept or decline message requests from new people
  • Set a group size limit (planned)
  • Control message saving (planned)

Screenshot notices apply to detected shortcuts. The web App cannot detect phone screenshots.

My profile and workspace

Your profile. Your work. Your choice.

Your Oneway profile brings your identity, connections, and experiences together in one customizable space. Choose how it looks and what people can see.

Workplace identity is optional. It keeps professional connections and activity separate from your personal life while you continue to use one Oneway account.
