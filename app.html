App

Five places, one dock.

Home, Discovery, Create, Messages and Me sit along the bottom of the App. Workspace joins them when you work at a Center.

Open the AppSign in

Home

For You, and you get to choose.

Start on a ranked For You feed, switch to Following, or pin custom feeds beside them that shape what you see.

Posts you import from supported connected platforms arrive on Home as themselves, and you arrange the Popits around them.

Discovery

The places on Oneway, on one map.

Open a place from the map to see its page and its events, follow it, or join one of its communities.

Create

Capture it. Import it. Make it yours.

Take a photo or a video, upload one, or import a post from a supported platform. Then name it and choose where it goes.

What you post goes out to a community you belong to. A document stays in your Workspace, seen by nobody, until you decide to share it.

Messages

Messages, Your Way.

Most messaging apps decide how your conversations work. Oneway lets you and your groups choose the rules.

Choose privacy, membership, posting, and notifications. An admin can manage the chat, or members can vote by majority, two thirds, or unanimous agreement.

Explore messaging controls
  • Keep messages or set them to disappear
  • Turn notices for detected screenshot shortcuts on or off
  • Choose who can add or remove people, post, and change rules
  • Open the group, approve requests, or invite people in
  • Let new members see the chat history or start fresh
  • Show or hide edit history and deleted message markers
  • Turn read receipts, typing, reactions, replies, and forwarding on or off
  • Allow photos and videos or keep the chat text only
  • Set slow mode or leave it off
  • Get every alert, mentions only, or mute the chat
  • Accept or decline message requests from new people

Screenshot notices apply to detected shortcuts. The web App cannot detect phone screenshots.

Me

Your profile. Your look. Every device.

Me is your profile and your settings. Choose a galaxy or a plain background, Popit mode or Simple mode, and a banner that fades behind the top of the page or sits framed below it.

The look you choose follows your account to every device you sign in on.

When you work at a Center, Workspace shows its work for your role, from the same account.

See it for yourself.
