App

Go anywhere. Connect everywhere.

Follow your friends and the places you love. The App greets you by name and shows what the people and places you follow are posting, as cards or full screen.

Discovery

Find people and places on the map.

Search for places, events and people near you or anywhere on Earth, and choose whether anyone sees your location.

Centers

Get to know the places you go.

Each place can show its posts, events, people and its own website, and you can follow, join or message it.

Events

Say you're going.

Reply Going, Maybe or Not going, see how many people are going, and add the event to your Home.

Messages

Message friends and places from one list.

Write to a friend or to a place you plan to visit. A visitor can message a place without an account.

Lightbulb

The camera opens first.

Add photos, videos or a link, then post to your profile or to a place you belong to.

My Center

Choose how your profile looks.

Choose your picture, colour, @name and bio, and add links to your other profiles, each shown with its real icon.
