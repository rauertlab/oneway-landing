Mission

Oneway brings the web and local community together.

A place for individual expression, stronger communities and more connected workplaces, shaped by the people who use it.

Ecosystem

We give every person one profile and every community or business a Center.

What you create, display, and how you interact is entirely up to you.

Your profile

One profile brings your digital presence together in one place.

A Center

Find a place, follow its updates or build a space for a community.

Canvas

Every Center is a canvas.

A Center is made of Popits: its posts, its events, its links, even its own website. Its team adds them in Oneway OS, moves and sizes them, and everyone who visits sees the Center exactly as it was made.

Compatibility & Integration

Bring the web together without leaving it behind.

Oneway is designed to connect websites, social platforms and business tools. Cross Projection keeps one Oneway record connected across its supported views, so an event can appear in the App, its Center and a connected website.

Connections are built platform by platform. Availability depends on each service's supported capabilities and permissions.

Privacy

Your identity and data stay under your control.

Choose what your profile shows and keep personal and workplace activity separate. Access to organizational work follows permissions, not a public profile.

Oneway OS

Building the best social media ecosystem for businesses and communities.

A public Center is where people connect with a place. Oneway OS is where its team can manage that presence and organize the work behind it.

Oneway OS and Developer OS are free. Business OS is $330 a month for one Center, by contact.
